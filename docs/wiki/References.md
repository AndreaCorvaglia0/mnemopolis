Fuller notes, with the strength of each support, are in

**Curiosity**
- Loewenstein, G. (1994). The psychology of curiosity: a review and reinterpretation. *Psychological Bulletin* 116(1).
- Kang, M. J., et al. (2009). The wick in the candle of learning. *Psychological Science* 20(8). https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/WickCandleLearning.pdf
- Recht, S., & Yeung, N. (2026). Knowability predicts curiosity and learning. https://www.psy.ox.ac.uk/people/nicholas-yeung/publication_modal/2440158

**Understanding**
- Chi, M. T. H., & Wylie, R. (2014). The ICAP framework. *Educational Psychologist* 49(4). https://doi.org/10.1080/00461520.2014.965823
- Brod, G., Hasselhorn, M., & Bunge, S. (2018). When generating a prediction boosts learning. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2018.01.013
- Martella, A. M., et al. (2023). *Educational Psychology Review*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09826-1
- Schroeder, N. L., et al. (2018). Studying and constructing concept maps: a meta-analysis. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-017-9403-9
- Hattie, J., & Timperley, H. (2007). The power of feedback. *Review of Educational Research* 77(1).

**Memory**
- Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-enhanced learning. *Psychological Science* 17(3).
- Dunlosky, J., et al. (2013). Improving students' learning with effective learning techniques. *Psychological Science in the Public Interest* 14(1).
- Twomey, C., & Kroneisen, M. (2021). The effectiveness of the loci method as a mnemonic device. https://doi.org/10.1177/1747021821993457

**Gamification**
- Sailer, M., & Homner, L. (2020). The gamification of learning: a meta-analysis. *Educational Psychology Review* 32(1). https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w
- Deci, E. L., Koestner, R., & Ryan, R. M. (1999). A meta-analytic review of experiments examining the effects of extrinsic rewards on intrinsic motivation. *Psychological Bulletin* 125(6).
