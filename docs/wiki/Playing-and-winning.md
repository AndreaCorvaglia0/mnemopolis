# Playing and winning

## What you are asked to do

1. Visit the houses in the order of their numbers.
2. In each, do the lab: predict, try, see why.
3. Challenge the host to a duel and win their badge.
4. With every badge of the main path, enter the League and win.
5. The road out of town opens. That is the end of the game: about an hour.

Then stay, if you like. The town does not close.

## How you know what to do next

- The **tracker** in the corner of the town always names the next step of the main path.
- The **houses are numbered** in the order of the learning path.
- **People** in the square tell you where to go; your character **thinks aloud** about it.
- The **menu** has three tabs: your Journey (the goal and what is left), the Map (what you have
  explored) and Mysteries (what you have found and what you carry).

## How you know you have won

The champion of the League steps aside, the gate opens, and an ending card sums up the big idea,
what you can now do, how you answered, and how many things in town are still a question mark.

## How to use your hands

| Gesture | For | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Tap, or Space | what matters: people, doors, labs, reading, barriers | a dialogue or a page |
| Double tap, or T | atmosphere: a thought about what you see | a thought bubble that fades |
| Walking near | animals and small happenings | nothing on screen |
| Exploring | secrets | a line in your discoveries |

## What is optional

Everything beyond the five steps above: secrets, upper floors, hidden buildings, the tall grass,
the shop, the cat with the lantern. A course can switch each of them off.
