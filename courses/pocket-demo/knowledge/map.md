# Concept map

```
wave (a travelling squeeze) ──requires── pitch (frequency, wavelength)
      │                                      │ contrasts
      ├──requires── loudness (decibels) ─────┘
      └──requires── medium (what sound travels through)
```

| Concept | Type | Wrong belief corrected | Station(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| wave | mechanism | the air flies from the speaker to the ear | Push the air (custom) |
| pitch | mechanism | a higher note travels faster, or has longer waves | More per second (pattern from code), Guess the curve (custom, the wow) |
| loudness | mechanism | decibels add like metres | Ten times (ready compare) |
| medium | cases | sound crosses empty space; dense solids are slow | Sort the mediums (ready sort) |

Path: wave first (everything rests on it), then pitch, loudness, medium. All four are core; the
League asks 6 questions of levels 2 and 3.
