# Brief: Chroma Town

- **Subject:** how colour works, on screens, on paper and in the eye.
- **Learner:** a curious adult with no background in colour science, who uses screens and printers every day.
- **Goals.** At the end the learner can:
  1. predict the result of mixing coloured lights;
  2. predict the result of mixing inks, the mirror image of mixing lights (taught by the Print
     Works, an extra: `"concepts": ["print"]`, so the ending card claims it only for players who did it);
  3. describe a colour with hue, saturation and lightness and relate it to RGB;
  4. explain why three numbers are enough to describe a colour, and why the same numbers can look different.
- **Typical mistakes:** treating every mixture like paint ("more colours, darker"); believing a screen needs a lamp for each colour; trusting the eye as a measuring instrument.
- **Size:** small (6 concepts).
- **Care:** standard.
- **Level:** introductory. One advanced station upstairs (Three Lamps House) and three locks.
- **Core** (the main story, about an hour): lamps, wheel, context, cones: light adds up, three dials describe it, the eye compares, and three signals are all it keeps. Print (ink, the mirror image of light) and the history (the same idea told in time) are extras, left for the curious so that the core stays within an hour.
- **Decision (Nov 2026): print stays extra; the big idea and the goals were made to match the core.**
  The big idea used to say "three lights on a screen, three inks on paper, three signals in the
  eye", and goal 1 included mixing inks, while print was not on the main path: the ending card
  claimed what the core never taught. Making print core again was tried on paper first. The
  validator's estimate of the core path is 60 minutes now; print adds 4.5 minutes for the house
  and 6.5 per ground-floor station. Its three stations cannot move upstairs, because a process is
  drawn as a workshop and a workshop has no upper floor (marking one `advanced` would make it
  unreachable). With all three it would be about 80 minutes, with one about 70; dropping another
  concept instead would break the story (cones carry "three signals in the eye" and goal 4,
  context carries "the same numbers can look different"). So the least damage was the other way:
  the big idea now reads "Colour is three numbers: three lights on a screen, three signals in the
  eye.", goal 1 is about lights only, and inks have their own goal mapped to `print`. The core
  path stays at about 60 minutes and the ending card claims only what the player did.
- **Questions:** six per core concept, so that the duels separate a learner who knows the idea
  from one who guesses (`skills/knowledge-base/references/6-questions.md`). Every trap is a
  sentence the learner reads after a wrong answer.
- **Language:** English.
- **Material received:** none. This is the example course of the repository, written from general reference knowledge (see `sources.md`).
